**Subject: Weekly COVID-19 Surveillance Forecast Summary – Bay Area**

The current forecast indicates a significant upward trajectory for COVID-19 cases across the entire nine-county San Francisco Bay Area. All monitored counties are projected to see rising case numbers over the next four weeks, with no signs of stabilization in the immediate future. Alameda and San Francisco counties are expected to experience the most substantial increases, both in terms of total case volume and the speed of growth.

The "uncertainty range" (prediction interval) provided in this forecast represents the margin of error for our estimates. A wide range, such as the one seen in Alameda, indicates that while we expect a surge, the exact number of cases could vary significantly. For decision-makers, a wide interval suggests we should prepare for a broad spectrum of possibilities—ranging from moderate growth to a much sharper spike—whereas a narrower interval allows for more precise planning of staff and supplies. It is important to note that because our current uncertainty ranges are somewhat narrow, we should remain vigilant for fluctuations that may exceed these estimates.

A key advantage of this model is the "lead-time" provided by wastewater monitoring. Because viral signals appear in our sewage systems before individuals begin showing symptoms or seeking medical care, these forecasts serve as an early warning system.
